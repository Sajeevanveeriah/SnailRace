========================================
  SNAIL RACE FUNDRAISER - QUICK START
  Newcomb & District Cricket Club
========================================

RUN IT (30 SECONDS)
===================

1. Keep index.html, style.css and script.js together in one folder.
2. Double-click "index.html".
3. Press F for full screen. Press M for the controls. Press SPACE to race.

No installation. No internet. No accounts.


THE FIVE KEYS THAT MATTER
=========================

  SPACE ....... Start the race (also dismisses the winner card)
  M ........... Show / hide the moderator controls
  ESC ......... Reset the race, or close the winner card
  F ........... Full screen
  CTRL+Z ...... Undo the last donation

Shortcuts are ignored while you're typing, so you can name a snail
"Shell Warne" without accidentally starting a race.

  Also: N = day/night stage, C = calm mode, S = sound on/off


BEFORE THE EVENT
================

Press M to open the controls, then:

  RACERS ...... Choose 4-8 snails and name them. Player names, sponsor
                businesses, in-jokes. "Suggest names" fills them with
                cricket puns if you're stuck.
  TARGET ...... Set tonight's goal so the bar on screen means something.
  RACE ........ Pick a race length. 10 seconds is the sweet spot.

Run one practice race and check it looks right on the projector.


DURING THE EVENT
================

TAKING A DONATION
  Pick the snail -> type the backer's name (optional) -> enter the
  amount, or tap $5 / $10 / $20 / $50 / $100 -> Add donation.

  Capturing the backer's name is optional, so never hold up a queue
  for it. But if you do, the winner's card names them on the big
  screen, and the club goes home with a list of people to thank.

MADE A MISTAKE?
  CTRL+Z undoes the last donation.
  "void" next to any ledger row reverses that one.
  Voided entries stay on the books marked VOID, so the numbers balance.

RACING
  Press SPACE. Countdown, race, winner card with the podium and the
  backers' names. It clears itself after ~15 seconds, or press ESC.
  The tote board resets for the next race. The night total keeps going.


AT THE END OF THE NIGHT
=======================

In the "End of night" panel:

  OPEN REPORT ... Night total, every race with its podium and pot,
                  per-snail totals, the full donor list, and a sign-off
                  line for whoever counts the cash.
                  "Print / Save as PDF" prints just the report.
  EXPORT CSV .... The same data as a spreadsheet.
  SAVE BACKUP ... A .json file you can restore on any machine.

Take a backup or a CSV before you close the laptop.


"IS IT RIGGED?"
===============

No, and you can prove it standing there.

The finishing order is drawn the instant you press Start, before any
snail moves, and the seed it was drawn from is printed on screen. The
draw never looks at the donations. Every snail wins exactly 1 race in N.

If someone grumbles, copy the seed from the results list into
"Verify draw" in the End of night panel. It re-runs the draw and prints
the order - which will match what everyone just watched.

Announce it as a bit of fun and a raffle-style draw, not a contest of
snail ability.


BIG SCREEN SETUP
================

One laptop, mirrored to the TV or projector over HDMI. Press F for full
screen and keep the controls closed with M. Open them when you need to
take a donation, then close them again.

Dark hall? Press N. The track goes dark so a big white rectangle isn't
blinding the room.

DON'T try to run a second copy on a tablet as a "remote control" - each
copy keeps its own separate books. Mirror one screen instead.


TROUBLESHOOTING
===============

Q: The race won't start?
A: If the controls are closed, press SPACE. If they're open, click
   Start. Make sure you're not in a text field.

Q: A donation went in wrong?
A: CTRL+Z, or hit "void" on that row in the ledger.

Q: The screen is blinding in a dark room?
A: Press N for the night stage.

Q: The animation is making someone unwell?
A: Press C for calm mode. The races still run, the decoration stops.

Q: It says NOT SAVING?
A: Some browsers block storage for files opened directly. Use
   "Save backup" regularly, or serve the folder over http://localhost.

Q: Do we need internet?
A: No. Nothing is fetched, nothing is sent, everything stays on the
   device.
